## Confidence

### High

- dynamic notch surface
- widgets
- live actions
- files shelf
- scroll/swipe
- notchless support
- multiple monitors

### Medium

- exact widget inventory
- exact settings
- current media-source behavior
- exact LazyShelf semantics
- exact animation model

### Low until hands-on

- pixel measurements
- exact colors
- exact animation curves
- exact gesture thresholds
- private implementation details

## Research rule

Current hands-on observation outranks stale public descriptions.

When evidence conflicts, document the conflict rather than silently choosing a convenient interpretation.
