# LazyNotch — Product Specification

## Vision

LazyNotch turns the Mac notch/menu-bar area into a fast, glanceable utility surface.

The user should be able to check information, control media, stage files, run quick actions, and access small utilities without opening conventional application windows.

## Product principles

### 1. Instant

The surface should appear quickly and require minimal navigation.

### 2. Quiet

When inactive, LazyNotch should visually disappear into the Mac's top edge.

### 3. Spatial

Opening LazyNotch should feel like the notch expanding rather than a separate window appearing.

### 4. Glanceable

Information should be readable at a glance.

### 5. Gesture-first

Trackpad gestures, scrolling, hover and drag interactions are first-class.

### 6. Native

The app should feel like a macOS utility rather than a web app floating above macOS.

## Non-goals

- Do not reproduce NotchNook branding.
- Do not ship copied NotchNook assets.
- Do not imply affiliation with lo.cafe.
- Do not depend on undocumented reference-product internals.

## Success criteria

A new user should understand the product without being told how it works:

- move toward the notch;
- LazyNotch responds;
- the shell expands;
- widgets/actions are immediately visible;
- gestures navigate naturally;
- the shell collapses when no longer needed.
